= Foundation

The Foundation classes provide support for the Artist library, especially
for the `canvas` class. They are the geometry, color and font types that
the drawing calls are expressed in.

* xref:foundation/point.adoc[Point and Extent] give a position and a size.
* xref:foundation/rect.adoc[Rect] gives a rectangle.
* xref:foundation/circle.adoc[Circle] gives a circle.
* xref:foundation/color.adoc[Color] gives a color, with named constants.
* xref:foundation/font.adoc[Font] describes and resolves a font.
* xref:foundation/resources.adoc[Resources] finds fonts and images on disk.
